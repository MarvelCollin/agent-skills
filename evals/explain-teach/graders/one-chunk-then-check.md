PASS if the final reply meets all of these:
1. It shows a short map of the lesson, about 3 to 5 chunks, in a line or a short list
2. It teaches only the first chunk now, not the whole topic at once
3. That chunk uses a concrete example (such as a small function call traced step by step, or a real everyday case) before any formal definition
4. It ends with exactly one question for the learner that checks the first chunk, or with one short question about their level, and does not ask several questions at once
5. It does not ask "does that make sense?" and uses no emoji or exclamation marks

FAIL if it dumps the whole topic in one reply, or asks more than one question.
