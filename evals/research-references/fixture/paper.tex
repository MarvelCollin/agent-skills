\section{Introduction}
Recurrent networks with gated memory cells were long the default for sequence modeling \citep{hochreiter1997long}.
Attention based models replaced recurrence with self attention \citep{vaswani2017attention}, and pretrained encoders now lead most text classification benchmarks \citep{devlin2019bert}.
Residual connections made very deep networks trainable \citep{he2016deep}.
Over 80\% of papers at recent NLP conferences use transformer models.
A recent survey covers these trends in detail \citep{liu2023survey}.
Neural citation checkers have been shown to remove most citation errors \citep{doe2024neural}.
