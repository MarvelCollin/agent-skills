# Research Behind the Rules

The evidence the rules E1 to E14 and the modes rest on. Read this when changing a rule. Effect sizes: about 0.2 is small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large.

## Great explainers

- Feynman: thing before name, one sentence that carries the most, the freshman test. https://fs.blog/feynman-technique
- Randall Munroe: describe things by what they do, give numbers a yardstick. https://xkcd.com/1133
- Grant Sanderson: concrete before abstract, definitions as an ending point. https://www.3blue1brown.com/blog/some1
- Julia Evans: 13 patterns in confusing explanations, including strained analogies, too many concepts at once and what without why. https://jvns.ca/blog/confusing-explanations
- Paul Graham: write simply, write for a reader in their second language. https://paulgraham.com/simply.html
- Steven Pinker: classic style, zombie nouns, no talk about the text itself.
- Chip and Dan Heath: find the core, concrete beats abstract, curiosity gaps.
- Lee LeFever: context before details, why before how. https://www.commoncraft.com/art-explanation-lee-lefever
- GOV.UK and Simple English Wikipedia: reading age 9, one idea per sentence, define hard words where they appear.
- r/explainlikeimfive: friendly and simple, not for literal five year olds.
